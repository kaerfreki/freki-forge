# GGUF image & video results — ComfyUI on a 16 GB card

> Hand-assembled from a one-off session (not generated by a harness in this
> repo). Raw records, including failed runs and re-runs:
> [`raw/gguf-image-video-2026-10-06-nvidia-geforce-rtx-4080.csv`](raw/gguf-image-video-2026-10-06-nvidia-geforce-rtx-4080.csv).

Measured on **2026-10-06** (LTX-2.5 rows on 2026-10-07):

- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4080, 16376 MiB (Windows driver 617.14) · RAM: 32 GB · OS: Windows 11
- ComfyUI 0.39.0 + [ComfyUI-GGUF](https://github.com/city96/ComfyUI-GGUF), KJNodes;
  stable-diffusion.cpp `master-929-3f8527a` (CUDA 12) for the `sd.cpp` rows
- **Single runs**, wall-clock per job including text encoding and VAE decode.
  "Cold" includes model load; "warm" is an immediate repeat.

Compare with [`RESULTS-images.md`](RESULTS-images.md) (SDXL / FLUX.1 on
Linux) for trends only — different OS, resolution and models.

## Takeaways

- **Diffusion is compute-bound on this card**: Q4 and Q8 take the same time
  (FLUX.2 6 s warm, Wan 5B ~310 s). A smaller quant buys VRAM/RAM headroom,
  not speed.
- **System RAM is the real limit for 14B+ video**: almost every 14B/22B run
  peaked at 30–32 GB of 32 GB. At 720p, Wan 14B Q6_K paged (538.8 s vs
  384.1 s for Q4_K_M), so Q4_K_M is the practical choice with 32 GB.
- **GGUF isn't always the fastest path**: ComfyUI's native int8 Qwen-Image
  build was 1.6–2.9× faster warm than the GGUFs, and 4–7× faster than
  stable-diffusion.cpp on edits.
- The unsloth Qwen-Image 2.1 GGUFs fail in stock ComfyUI-GGUF
  ("Unknown model architecture"); their rows were produced later with a
  different GGUF loader build.
- A 4-step distillation LoRA (lightx2v) cut Wan 14B 480p from 922.8 s
  (20 steps) to ~130 s.

## Caveats

- Single runs, not medians; several configs were re-run after fixes and the
  table shows the **last successful** run (all attempts, including errors,
  are in the raw CSV).
- ComfyUI's dynamic-VRAM feature was turned off part-way through the session
  after a crash (before the Qwen-Image GGUF rows), so not every row ran
  with identical ComfyUI settings.
- Model downloads and other jobs shared the disk/RAM during parts of the
  session, which mostly affects cold loads.
